FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a website take?

Simple sites usually take one to two weeks. Larger websites and apps take longer depending on features. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

Do I need to provide content?

It helps, but it's not required. We can guide you on what to write and which images to use, or work with content you already have.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every site we build is fully responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can you improve my existing website?

Absolutely. We can redesign, speed up or add features to a site you already have.

How do payments work?

We typically agree on a deposit to begin and the balance on delivery. Larger projects can be split into milestones.
